faq

questions, answered.

do i really need no laptop?

right. treena runs the whole toolchain on your phone — terminal, editor, and deploys. the laptop becomes optional, not required.

which languages and tools work?

node, python, go, rust, ruby and the usual suspects — plus git, ssh, and your package managers. if it runs in a shell, it runs in treena.

does it work offline?

editing and local runs work offline; pushes and deploys sync the moment you're back on a connection.

is my code private?

your code is yours. treena doesn't read it, train on it, or share it.
